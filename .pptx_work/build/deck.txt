How to verify or benchmark?

•   Layered verification: Check user intent, formulation,
    implementation, and solution feasibility/optimality—not just solver
    success.
•   New benchmarks: Combine expert-reviewed components, hand-
    solvable cases, and mathematical property tests (e.g., relaxing
    constraints cannot worsen the optimum).
•   Verifier evaluation: Inject missing constraints, sign errors, and
    incorrect device mappings; supplement with cross-tool comparisons
    and expert review.
